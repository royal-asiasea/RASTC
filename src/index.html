About Us

Royal Asia Sea Trade Corporation was first operated under the name of NJM Corporation. After two years of operation since 2015, the company changed its name at the start of 2017 and specialized in the trading of frozen fish, all kinds of frozen seafood, and frozen meat products.

Our yard area covers 3,000 square meters and has more than 20 employees. Our products are 100 percent imported from Asian countries.
